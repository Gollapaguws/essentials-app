# Essentials: One Simple Place for Every Event

**Essentials** brings events, live moments, payments, and community together in one easy-to-use place — on the web and on your phone.

## The big idea

Putting on an event, or being part of one, should feel exciting — not complicated. Essentials is an all-in-one home for events where organizers can set things up, people can join in, and everyone can stay connected. Whether an event happens in a room full of people, online, or a bit of both, Essentials makes it simple to bring it all together.

## The problem

Today, running or joining an event often means jumping between different tools: one place to sell tickets, another to go live, another to take payments, and yet another to keep people in the loop. It's confusing, time-consuming, and can shut out people who aren't "tech-savvy." Organizers and venues work harder than they should, and attendees miss out on feeling truly connected — especially when they can't be there in person.

## A real-life picture

Imagine **Maya**, who hosts small music nights at a local café. Planning used to be a headache — she needed separate tools for tickets and messages, and had no easy way for friends across town to join.

With Essentials, Maya sets up her music night in one place. Neighbors can find the event, buy a ticket, and get the details on their phone or computer. On the night of the show, people at the café enjoy the performance while friends who couldn't make it watch live and feel part of the moment as it happens. Afterward, Maya gets paid through the same place she used to run everything — without bouncing between apps.

## How Essentials makes it simple

Essentials puts the pieces together so people don't have to:

- **Create and run events** in one place, without a patchwork of tools.
- **Bring people together as it happens** with live, real-time experiences — so those at home can share in the moment alongside those in the room.
- **Handle payments the easy way**, like selling tickets and getting paid, built right in.
- **Build community** around each event, helping organizers, attendees, and venues stay connected.

## Who it's for

- **Event organizers** (artists, hosts, promoters, and businesses) get an easy way to set up events, reach people, go live, and get paid — without needing to be technical.
- **Attendees and fans** get a simple way to discover, join, and enjoy events, and to feel part of what's happening in real time, in person or from afar.
- **Venues** get tools to host and support events smoothly and to connect with organizers and audiences.

## Why Essentials is different

Many tools do one piece of the job. Essentials is different because it focuses on **simplicity**: it brings in-person and live experiences together with built-in payments and community, all in one place. It's designed so everyday people — not just technical experts — can create and enjoy great events.

## Use it anywhere

Essentials works **on the web and on your phone**, so organizers and attendees can take part wherever they are.

## How Essentials sustains itself

Essentials is built to support itself in a straightforward, fair way:

- **A small fee on tickets or payments:** When tickets are sold or payments are processed through Essentials, a small slice goes to Essentials for handling it, and the organizer keeps the rest. That means Essentials does well when organizers have successful events.
- **Simple monthly plans:** Organizers or venues who run events often can choose a recurring plan for extra tools that make planning and hosting even easier, with predictable costs.

Together, this mix lets occasional hosts get started easily while giving frequent organizers and venues options that grow with them. *(These are plain-language examples, not fixed prices.)*

## Our vision

Essentials imagines a world where anyone can bring people together — across a room or across town — and every event feels easy to create, easy to join, and truly shared in the moment.
