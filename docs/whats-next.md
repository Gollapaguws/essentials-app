# What's next

*Draft for sharing with clients. Order and scope may shift; no dates are promised here.*

We have just shipped slot-based nights, an hourly DJ fee, a hard event budget, completion-gated invoicing, the fixed R4.95/km travel rate and a DJ earnings view. Here is what we are picking up next.

| What's next | What it means for you | Where you'll see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slots, budget and pay in the mobile app** | Promoters can build and cap a night, and DJs can check what a slot pays, from a phone instead of the web dashboard. | Mobile app |
| **One travel rate for every DJ** | The standard rate is R4.95/km. Right now profiles created earlier can still carry their own rate, and the Settings screen reports the rate most DJs are on. Next step is moving every profile onto the single rate, so there is one number and no "mixed" state. | Settings, DJ profiles, invoices |
| **A different fee for a headline slot** | Today one hourly rate covers the whole night. Next, a single slot — the headline set — can pay a different rate without changing the rest of the plan. | Events → Slots & budget |
| **Remaining budget across all upcoming nights** | The budget view is per event. Next, one screen shows every upcoming night, what is committed and how much room is left before confirmations stop. | Events / Analytics |

## How we choose what comes next

- Anything that stops money going wrong first: an overspend, a double invoice, a fee nobody agreed to.
- Then anything that saves a promoter or DJ a manual step.
- Then presentation and reporting.

If something on this list is more urgent for your venue or roster than the order above, tell us and we will move it up.
