A quick update on how gigs work now.

**Promoters** — plan a night in back-to-back slots and set one DJ fee per hour; each slot pays that rate for its length. Set a budget: confirmations now stop at it, so a night can't overspend, and you can raise the budget later. After the set, confirm the gig happened before the DJ can invoice.

**DJs** — apply for a specific slot and see exactly what it pays. Invoicing opens once the promoter confirms you played. Your new Earnings view shows what completed gigs have earned. Travel is R4.95/km, set by ESSENTIALS.

**Venues** — DJ invoices arrive only after the promoter confirms the gig, at the agreed fee.

Full details are in the app under "What's new". Any questions, just reply.
